FAQ

Straight answers

Are you a Wix-only studio?

No. Wix Studio is a core platform because it is fast to ship and easy for clients to run. We also build Shopify stores, and we will say when a platform is the wrong fit.

Who will I actually work with?

A named lead from day one. Andreas takes strategy calls. Design, project management and build stay in-house — the team on this page is the team on the project.

Do you only work in Cyprus?

The studio is in Nicosia. Clients sit across Cyprus and Europe. Remote kickoffs are normal; on-site is available when it helps.

Can you rebuild an existing site?

Yes. Most of our launches started as sites that looked fine and did not perform. We keep what converts and replace what does not.
